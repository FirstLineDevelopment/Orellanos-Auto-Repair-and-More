## Visual Direction

- Overall visual concept: mobile mechanic dispatch page built from the logo and business card, not a generic shop website.
- Emotional impression: reachable, practical, mechanical, phone-first, working-class professional.
- Brand personality: bold, direct, mobile, owner-operated, truck-and-tools energy.
- Existing brand signals to preserve: blue Orellano wordmark, black base, silver/white lettering, mechanic/truck/tools illustration, phone number, service list.
- Authentic asset inventory reviewed: yes
- What authentic visual material can this design be built around? The logo and business card are the primary design inputs.
- Color strategy and rationale: black/near-black base, vivid mechanic blue, cool silver, white, and restrained red only where inherited from the logo/tool accent.
- Typography strategy and rationale: heavy compact sans-serif to echo the bold block lettering on the card, with smaller utility labels for service/phone actions.
- Imagery importance: image-critical
- Photography/imagery strategy: use the actual logo and business-card screenshot as concept assets; request original files for production; avoid generic generated garage hero as the main identity.
- Hero strategy: brand/logo-led hero with mobile mechanic headline, not a full-bleed shop photo.
- Hero authentic-image decision: use supplied logo as the hero visual because it contains the business positioning and palette.
- Page composition strategy: asymmetrical logo hero, quick fact strip, mobile-service workflow, dark service-board grid, coverage/hours panel, dark request dock, compact final CTA.
- Section rhythm strategy: distinct structural modules rather than the previous Mike-like sequence of hero, three cards, editorial split, red band, form, dark footer.
- Whitespace/depth/layering/texture strategy: mechanical grid texture, logo ring echo, service-board lines, clean phone CTA, strong contrast.
- CTA hierarchy: Call `(203) 706-5687` primary; request form and Facebook secondary.
- Signature visual element(s): large Orellano Mobile Mechanic logo in hero, blue service-board rail, coverage/hours panel.
- Mobile visual strategy: phone-first, logo visible early, no clipped hero text, service list as large tap targets, compact footer.
- Brand specificity test answer: Pass for concept. Replacing the name/logo would break the design because the layout, palette, copy, and service structure now depend on Orellano's actual logo and card.
- Known visual compromises: attached logo/card are screenshots from Facebook, not original production artwork; final owner permission and original files are still required.
